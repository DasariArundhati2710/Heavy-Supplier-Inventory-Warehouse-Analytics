# Sprint 1 Report
## Heavy Supplier, Inventory & Warehouse Analytics

### 1. Sprint Objective
To understand the available datasets, inspect data quality, prepare cleaned datasets, and identify key relationships for supplier, inventory, purchasing, and sales analysis.

### 2. Work Completed
- Reviewed 12 source CSV datasets.
- Prepared cleaned datasets.
- Performed data quality checks.
- Generated supplier performance and inventory risk analysis outputs.
- Generated monthly, branch-wise, and product-wise sales summaries.
- Checked purchase-order header totals against purchase-order line totals.
- Started building the Power BI dashboard.

### 3. Key Findings
- Inventory stock values exceed maximum-stock levels across all 180 product-branch combinations and require investigation.
- Duplicate invoice IDs and payment IDs were identified and require further review.
- Purchase-order header totals reconcile with line-item totals within the checked tolerance.
- Supplier performance and sales summaries are available for further analysis.

### 4. Pending Work
- Investigate inventory stock discrepancies.
- Review duplicate invoice and payment records.
- Complete supplier performance visualizations.
- Continue building the Power BI report.

### 5. Sprint Outcome
Initial data preparation and analysis have been completed. The findings will support further investigation and dashboard development in the next sprint.
