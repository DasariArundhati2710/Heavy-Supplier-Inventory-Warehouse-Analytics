## 3. Key Findings
- Inventory stock exceeds maximum-stock levels across all 180 product-branch combinations and requires investigation.
- Duplicate invoice IDs and payment IDs require further review.
- Missing received dates were found for 2,370 purchase orders, corresponding to cancelled orders in the reviewed data.
- Purchase-order header totals reconcile with line-item totals within the checked tolerance.
